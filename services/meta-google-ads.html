FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Meta & Google Ads FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about our media buying and scaling methodology.

We balance demand capture and demand creation. Google captures existing high-intent search demand (users actively searching for your product or related search terms), while Meta unlocks scalable cold audience discovery through viral hooks and algorithmic feed distribution. We continuously rebalance budget based on blended Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER) and inventory velocity.

Creative fatigue is the single biggest threat to paid media profitability. We test between 8 to 14 new creative assets every week across diverse angles (direct response problem-solution, unboxing, user testimonials, founder story, and comparison charts) inside dedicated dynamic creative testing (DCT) sandbox campaigns.

We implement direct server-to-server tracking via Meta Conversions API (CAPI) and Google Ads Enhanced Conversions through cloud gateway servers. This restores high Event Quality Match (EQM) ratings (8.5+/10), feeding the ad algorithms complete conversion telemetry to optimize bidding accurately.

We typically recommend a minimum monthly media budget of ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 ($2,500 - $6,000+) across Meta and Google. This budget ensures enough daily conversion events to reach algorithmic statistical significance rapidly during creative and audience testing phases.
