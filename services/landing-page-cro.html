FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Landing Page & CRO FAQs

Answers to essential questions regarding our landing page design and conversion rate optimization process.

Standard eCommerce product pages carry dozens of exit distractions (navigation bars, unrelated menu links, category banners) and lack cohesive direct-response storytelling. Dedicated landing pages and advertorials guide prospects through a focused narrative tailored specifically to the ad hook they clicked, routinely lifting conversion rates by 30% to 60%.

We work seamlessly with your existing eCommerce tech stack without disruption. For Shopify brands, we build using Replo, Instant Commerce, or custom headless Next.js pages that plug directly into your native Shopify checkout, payment gateways, and inventory management systems.

We use rigorous Bayesian and Frequentist statistical significance models, requiring a minimum of 95% confidence level before calling a winner. We track primary conversion rate, Average Order Value (AOV), and Revenue Per Visitor (RPV) to ensure that the winning variation drives genuine bottom-line profit.

Our typical sprint delivery is 7 to 10 days from project kickoff to live traffic for your first bespoke landing page. This includes comprehensive audience research, direct-response copywriting, custom UI/UX design in Figma, responsive development, and QA tracking verification.
